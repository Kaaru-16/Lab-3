Sandok

In Filipino culture, pag-sasandok (the act of ladling or serving food) goes far beyond a simple dining courtesy, a profound, non-verbal expression of affection and care. pag-sasandok is a quiet, everyday way of saying, "I am looking out for you, and I want to make sure you are warm, full, and taken care of."

At Sandok, we believe that the simplest acts of care are found at the dining table. Founded by Chef Elvie, our kitchen was born from her lifelong philosophy that pag-sasandok, the act of serving others, is the purest expression of love. Drawing from her family's heirloom recipes, Chef Elvie created Sandok to recreate the authentic warmth of a Filipino home, ensuring every meal we ladle into your bowl feels like a comforting embrace.
