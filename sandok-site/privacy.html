Privacy policy.

We collect only what we need to welcome you well, respond to your questions, and manage your reservations.

01

Information we collect

When you reserve a table or contact us, we may collect your name, email address, telephone number, dining preferences, accessibility needs, and any details you choose to share. We may also collect basic technical information such as browser type and pages visited.

02

How we use it

We use this information to manage reservations, respond to messages, accommodate dining requirements, improve our service, maintain site security, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

03

When information is shared

Information may be shared with service providers that help us operate reservations, email, and website hosting. These providers may use it only to perform services for Sandok. We may also disclose information when required by law.

04

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. You can also ask us to stop sending non-essential communications. Some records may be retained where required for operational or legal purposes.

05

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@sandok.com. We aim to respond within thirty days.
