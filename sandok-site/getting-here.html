By car

Limited street parking is available. Paid parking is located two blocks away at Salcedo Car Park.

By public transit

From Ayala MRT, take the Washington jeepney and alight at Tordesillas Street.

Accessibility

Our main entrance and dining room are step-free. Contact us for any additional assistance.

Landmark

We are one block south of Salcedo Saturday Market, across from the neighborhood bakery.
