Why we serve

Pag-sasandok is an act of care.

In Filipino culture, ladling food into someone’s bowl is a quiet way of saying, “I’m looking out for you.”

Chef Elvie built Sandok around that simple gesture. Her kitchen brings family recipes to the table with honest ingredients and the same generosity found at home.
